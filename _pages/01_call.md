#### MMAI Call for Papers

We invite submissions to the 5th IEEE International Workshop on **Multimodal AI (MMAI)**, held in conjunction with the IEEE International Conference on Data Mining (ICDM) 2025, taking place in Washington, D.C., USA, from November 12–15, 2025.

The MMAI workshop serves as a dedicated forum for researchers and practitioners to present cutting-edge advancements and practical solutions in multimodal artificial intelligence. It fosters rich exchange of ideas and promotes collaboration through insightful discussions and presentations.

We welcome original research papers related to the workshop’s themes, including but not limited to multimodal representation learning, multimodal data modeling, data fusion techniques, and multimodal learning frameworks.

#### Submission Policy

Please follow [IEEE manuscript templates (Overleaf or US Letter)](https://www.ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates) and [IEEE reference guide](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/7/IEEE_Reference_Guide.pdf) to format your paper, and then directly submit to [MMAI@ICDM paper submission site](https://www.wi-lab.com/cyberchair/2025/icdm25/scripts/submit.php?subarea=S19&undisplay_detail=1&wh=/cyberchair/2025/icdm25/scripts/ws_submit.php).

The submissions must be in PDF format without author list (double-blind), written in English, and formatted according to the IEEE publication camera-ready style. All the paper review follows double-blind peer review.

Accepted papers will be published in the [IEEE ICDM Workshop proceedings](https://www3.cs.stonybrook.edu/~icdm2025/index.html).
