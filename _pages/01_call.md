### Call for Papers – MMAI 2025

We invite submissions to the **5th IEEE International Workshop on Multimodal AI (MMAI)**, held in conjunction with the **IEEE International Conference on Data Mining (ICDM) 2025**, taking place in **Washington, D.C., USA, from November 12–15, 2025**.

MMAI serves as a premier forum for academic researchers and industry practitioners to present the latest advancements and emerging trends in **multimodal artificial intelligence**. The workshop promotes collaboration and knowledge sharing through presentations, discussions, and networking.

We welcome high-quality, original research contributions related to, but not limited to, the following topics:
- Multimodal representation learning  
- Multimodal data modeling  
- Multimodal data fusion techniques  
- Multimodal learning frameworks and architectures  
- Cross-modal retrieval and reasoning  
- Applications of multimodal AI in healthcare, robotics, transportation, education, etc.

### Submission Tracks

We are accepting submissions in two categories:

- **Short Papers**:  
  5–6 pages (main content) + up to 4 pages for references and appendix

- **Poster Papers**:  
  3–4 pages (main content) + up to 4 pages for references and appendix

Papers must be submitted in **PDF format**, written in **English**, and formatted according to the [IEEE conference camera-ready style](https://www.ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates). All submissions will be peer-reviewed and selected based on originality, technical quality, and relevance to the workshop.

#### Double-blind review policy:  
Please remove author names and affiliations from your manuscript before submission.
